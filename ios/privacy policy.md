# Privacy Policy for Transport Alarm
Last updated: 20/10/2026

Transport Alarm has no accounts and doesn’t collect personal data. Your alarms, stops and settings are stored only on your device. The app doesn’t use analytics, advertising or tracking.

iOS alarms: to wake your alarms in the background, the app sends our server (hosted on Cloudflare) your device’s Apple push notification token and the times your alarm needs to check arrivals. This contains no name, account, stop or route. It’s deleted once the alarm finishes or is turned off, or when Apple reports the token is no longer valid.

Location: if you allow it, your location is used only on your device to centre the map. It isn’t sent to us.

Third-party services: maps are provided by Google Maps, and timetables and live arrivals are fetched directly from the transport operators and open data services (such as TfL, the UK Bus Open Data Service and Hong Kong operators). These services receive standard request information such as your IP address, and their own privacy policies apply.

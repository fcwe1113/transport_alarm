# Known Issues
- Currently Scotland and Wales have spotty coverage by the app, this is because UK Bus Open Data Service, the main data source currently in use does not cover Scotland and Wales in legislation like England does, meaning they mostly do their own thing there. Down the line I have plan to try and plug the gap, however it depends on whether individual bus companies do allow me to use their individual API, assuming there is one
## BODS data
- Currently does not respect diversions, the app will try to map the diverted bus using the original route, resulting in large estimation changes, effects worsen the closer you are to the diversion
